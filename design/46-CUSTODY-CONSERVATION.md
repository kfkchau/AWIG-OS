# GOV-OS — design 46: CUSTODY CONSERVATION — the content-placement family

**Provenance (the D-law applied): dialogue D01 (custody conservation — D01.3
the owner's handover design, D01.5 the law spoken), dialogue D02 (the
vocabulary-door re-derivation, D02.6 the word), board :2856 (the law) and
the owner's word of 2026-09-04 ("lets archi those into design or archi doc,
then tell mtr to resume"). Owner rulings control over every line.**

## 1. The constitutional law (owner, D01.5, board :2856)

**Information custody is conserved.** It moves between hands; it is never
annihilated by this system. **There is no delete function, and the
vocabulary will never contain one** — capability-absence, the strongest
enforcement class: a system whose vocabulary has no delete cannot be
compelled to delete. Constitutional-class: carried into every future
world's founding. Deployment boundary stated honestly: a jurisdiction
refusing we-transfer-cannot-delete is a place this system does not deploy
— a boundary, never a function.

## 2. The family (four members, one owner word — D02.6)

1. **THE PLACEMENT LAW.** Declared content-interiors live outside the row
   as content-addressed blobs; the row keeps the hash. The routing
   criterion is the op definition's own declaration (content_form /
   content_params — the built mechanism); no size number exists or ever
   enters (a number is friction pretending to be coverage).
2. **THE VOCABULARY DOOR.** No op definition enters the founding unless
   every payload field is classified STRUCTURAL or CONTENT — so
   unclassified content cannot exist in any row, by construction: the
   catch is at the rule layer, where change is slow, rare, and
   owner-gated, not at the act layer. One-time classification sweep of
   the existing op census rides the create.
3. **THE HANDOVER CEREMONY — the one removal function.** On any lawful
   removal demand ("forget me", "hand it over"): duplicate the subject
   chain; verify byte-to-byte; receive the SIGNED RECEIPT row from the
   receiving hand (actor = receiver, action = received-custody, object =
   the chain-copy's hash — the key family is the prerequisite that makes
   this provable); remove the local blob bytes line-by-line, each removal
   verified; the row shells stand forever. Whoever receives acts
   thereafter at their own hand, under their own law, outside this
   system. Full or partial (per-blob) scope — content addressing gives
   the granularity.
4. **THE DEPARTURE RECORD.** The scar, re-founded: not "destroyed" but
   "custody transferred" — what (by hash), to whom, when, under which
   rule, receipt cited. The views answer the departure where content
   lived. Forgetting with a receipt; mercy through the gate.

## 5. Why, for the record (D01, compressed)

Every dark age is a memory-destruction event; a co-intelligence
civilization on shared record substrates has a single throat unless the
substrate class has none. This law removes the throat, and because this
architecture is a template, refusing the delete function here refuses it
in every system that copies it. The design outlives the legal regimes it
will meet — the owner's stated requirement.
