## 3d — LIFECYCLE HOOKS AS THE OWNER FRAMED THEM (A3, PROPOSED)

His question answered in the affirmative and sharpened: RULES ARE
PERMANENT BY DEFAULT — standing until amended or retired through the
door, which is the estate's current truth and the stable choice; a
whole law layer carrying compulsory expiry would make the law itself
perishable. THE HOOK DUTY BINDS THE EXCEPTION CLASS ONLY: a rule
minted as TEMPORARY, CONDITIONAL, or EXPERIMENTAL must declare its
review trigger or expiry IN ITS OWN CONTENT, and — mechanised per the
K2 precedent — a rule of the exception class missing its hook REFUSES
AT THE DOOR as malformed. Silence means permanent; the exception
speaks or it does not enter. The deferral schedule (laws scheduled at
C6) is this pattern's first live instance, retrofit-free.
