## 2. The essence (one paragraph, functional)

Campaign 4 made one record provable: every act chained, signed and attested, custody conserved, recovery never inverting the truth hierarchy. Campaign 5 makes **the box's border a governed thing**. Every socket act — open, bind, listen, connect, accept, close — becomes a recorded decision citing a rule, the live socket table a derived view, and per-packet traffic never a record. The firewall is rules in the record, not a table beside it. An outside process speaks through a **door** as an entity: its request is a draft the gate decides, the reply carries the crossing's id and a seal over what was shown, and a refusal reaches the human through the system's own window, never through the refused party. The two comms laws already created at 1.39.0 gain their enforcement. Views gain a servicing record so a mandated view never run is detectable, and the profile law's round trip is proven by founding a dummy TWC v0.1 from its own pack through the door. **The campaign's smallest behaviour is one row crossing the border with a receipt** — two records on two bodies, each body appending only to its own record, one pen per record held by construction (D08.30). That is how "one-machine work" (design/44 §4) and "two records on two bodies exchange one row" (charter horizon addendum) are the same claim made from each side of the border: C5 governs THIS box's side and proves it against a second body it does not trust, federate or write — the second body here is the guest, the same box's own VM, with the TWC dummy entity as the second body of the door. Nothing here forecloses C6. Real key material arrives first (P0) so that acts at the border are attributable by real signatures wherever the library is present.
