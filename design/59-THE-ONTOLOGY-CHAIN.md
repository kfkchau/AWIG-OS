## Part 1 — the primitives to rule, event, decision and governance (the owner's chain, his order, cleaned on his corrections of 2026-09-16)

```
PRIMITIVES
[item]        = a clear scopeable thing
[time]        = passing

COMPARISON (the primitive act; the lens is inside the comparison, not a term of its own)
multi-[item] [comparison]           >> [same] + [different]
[same]        = two [item]s identical in one [comparison]
[different]   = two [item]s not identical in one [comparison]
   (a same/different from another comparison is other information, never a contradiction)

CHANGE
[time] x multi-[item] [comparison]  >> [change] + [unchanged]
[change]      = [different] of one [item] across [time]
[unchanged]   = [same] of one [item] across [time]
[change] kind                       >> [transformation] + [move]
[transformation] = [change] of what the [item] IS                 [move] = [change] of WHERE the [item] is: a place, or a holder's hands

ACTOR (defined from the change it drives, never the change from it)
[actor]       = [item] that is [change] driving                                       (the owner's)
[non-actor]   = [item] that drives no [change]; it can still undergo [change]         (the owner's "unchanged in nature", cleaned)

ITEM FORMS (a form is what a comparison reports, not a property the item carries)
[item] form         >> [physicalness] + [consumability]
[physicalness]      >> [physical] <-> [virtual]              [virtual] = not bound to one body; copies at no loss
[consumability]     >> [consumable] <-> [non-consumable]     [non-consumable] = use does not use it up
[information]       = [item] in form [virtual] x [non-consumable]                     (the owner's)
   (so information cannot be removed from the world, only moved between holders: no delete, only handover)
[information] form  >> [structured] <-> [unstructured]       = how much of the reading is built into the item's own form (S/U)

ACTIVITY, OBSERVATION, RULE, EVENT (the owner's four lines, one definition each)
[activity]    = [information] about [change]: its [actor], its [action] (which transformation or move), its [object], its [target]     (the row)
[observation] = [transforming], sub-category creation, of [activity]                  (the pen; an observation is itself an activity, so the record holds its own writing)
[rule]        = [information] that is a requirement: should or should-not             (requirement is not a separate term)
[event]       = [activity] of [happened]

DRAFT, DECISION, GOVERNANCE (the owner's cuts)
[draft]       = [information] an [actor] created that awaits a [decision]; its status is derived later, never stored on it
[decision]    = [activity] that permanentises an [information] (a rule, or anything): from changeable to [unchanged]
[governance]  = [activity] with a [rule] as [object] or [target], by [actor]s whose permission reaches rules
[compliance]  = [same] of an [event] and the [rule] it falls under        [breach] = [different] of the two
```

The owner's corrections absorbed in this part, in order: an observer is an actor; the act defines the actor; nothing is a property of an item; driving and frame are not terms; observation has one definition, the creation of an activity; mode, requirement and view are struck as terms; a draft is created information awaiting a decision; a decision permanentises information; governance is any activity on a rule; the three CGL lines struck, then the one-line CGL and the three grammar lines struck too as restatements of Part 1 (his 2026-09-16 correction); CGL's remainder is two derived things in Part 2.

## Part 1b — the coordinate reading (the owner's projection, 2026-09-16: "when an activity creates a driver it inherits all rules related to driver"; generalised at his word to the whole chain)

Every split in Part 1 and every grammar in CGL is an AXIS with a closed set of values. An item established by an act sits at ONE place on every axis at once (actor|information · S|U · physical|virtual · consumable|non-consumable · move|transformation · single|organisation · one session|many · inside|outside the computer · ordinary|hardware-touching · ...). Every word ever used for a kind — driver, human, ai, program, rule, event, institution — is the NAME OF A CELL OR REGION in that one space, never a field on a row. A rule binds a REGION, never a name; an item inherits every rule whose region contains its place, by containment, the way the org tree already gives people their permissions. Same and different between two kinds are containment and overlap of regions: Venn2's five states read in the coordinate frame. CGL's comparability without merging is two rule systems describing regions in the same axes, laid over each other cell by cell.

```
[axis]        = one split of Part 1 or one CGL grammar, with a closed set of values, founded as vocabulary
[place]       = an [item]'s value on every [axis], read off its establishing row and its capability rows; never stored as a kind
[region]      = a set of places, named (driver, program, human, rule, event, institution ...)
[rule] reach  = the [region] a [rule] binds; an [item] inherits every [rule] whose region contains its [place]      (containment, Venn2 NS/EM in this frame)
[create X]    = CREATE-ACTOR or CREATE-INFO with the [place] declared (+ its capability rows); NEVER a new operation per kind
[compare] two rule systems = lay their regions over the same axes, cell by cell; no merging
```

Consequences: no type word on any row; a new kind is a new coordinate, not a new operation; a rule's reach is computed. Cost: the axes must be closed and declared (CGL's demand anyway) and every row must carry or derive its place. Considered today in three places, founded as one mechanism in none: the founding's driver as a system-class actor with a capability rule; C6's ceilings composing by intersection and classes nesting; C8's five-dimension matrix computed from rows (design/56 L5). Becomes campaign 8's first behaviour under the owner's words.

## Part 1c — THE ROOT OF THE INFORMATION TREE, the owner's (2026-09-17, his words: "I'd go activity record, atomic information item, and relationship to start with")

[information] >> [activity record] + [atomic information item] + [relationship]      (the record's three lists; nothing else is a fourth kind)

[activity record] = the row: actor, action, object, target (+ time when it happened). CORRECTED THE SAME HOUR ON THE OWNER'S WORD ("isnt rule in the aaot format? isnt that activity?" -- yes): the activity record splits into [rule] <-> [event]. A [rule] is activity in PATTERN form -- actor (a class or a place), action, object kind, target -- carrying a requirement (may / must / must not) and no time: a required activity. An [event] is activity in HAPPENED form -- a particular actor, action, object, target, and its time, decided or refused. The architect's earlier line 'a rule is an item' is struck: a rule is not content that stands alone, it is the activity shape itself with a modality; what makes it a rule is the requirement, what makes it a row is the shape. [governance] = activity with a rule as object or target (Part 1). Compliance / breach = same / different of an event against a rule (Part 1), which is only possible because both are the one shape.
[atomic information item] = a thing that holds content, one thing one place, versioned never edited; it stands alone. [draft], a program's sealed image, a device's identity, a view's stored copy are items by what their content is; a rule is not (it is activity in pattern form, above).
[relationship] = no content of its own: two ends and a kind (containment, custody, bind, handshake), one fact with two signed halves (venn2). It is what gives an item its PLACE — a region is read off relationships (Part 1b, L7) — so rules bind items through relationships.
   functional difference the owner asked: an item is the noun (content), a relationship is the position (where the noun sits among nouns); an activity is the verb with its time.

Derived, never a fourth root: [event] = a row; [rule] = activity in pattern form with a requirement; [draft] = an item awaiting a decision; [view] = a computed reading over the three lists; [actor] = an item that a load row has made an incarnation of and that acts only through its two openings — the stored program files are an item, never an actor (the owner, 2026-09-17: "program files stored etc is never an actor, i only become an actor that the system recognised when (1) the program actor rule table is loaded and (2) that the program operates with the rule table through the 2 channel opening glassbox" — yes, exactly; design/56 B1, B7, §3a).

## Part 2 — ten derived terms, one step each (rewritten 2026-09-16 on the owner's refusal of the first Part 2 as an inventory, not a derivation)

Binding line (the owner's correction of 2026-09-16): no term in this chain is a property of an item. Every equals sign is a comparison; every state word is derived over acts; a row is one act and its fields are the places items took in it, never attributes of a stored thing.

```
[record]        = every [observation] one [actor] made, in [time] order, only ever added         (the observer's memory; one pen because one observer)
[derived]       = [information] made by [comparison] over a [record], never observed directly       (gov-os calls one of these a view; delete it, recompute it, same)
[relationship]  = [comparison] of two [item]s, or of two [activity]s              (derived below, Part 2a, by splits; not one line)

[signature]     = [information] on a [row] that only the [actor] holding a [secret] could have created   (live identity moved into information form)

## Part 2a — two items: Venn2's five, and composition, derived by splits (the owner's correction of 2026-09-16: these were named, not derived)

```
TWO ITEMS IN PLACE (one [time], one [rule] read under; without a rule the comparison has no meaning)
two-[item] [comparison] on [place]   >> [no contact] + [contact]
[contact]                            >> [boundary only] + [shared interior]
[shared interior]                    >> [partial] + [one inside the other]
[one inside the other]               >> [at the edge] + [at the core]         (the edge is a width the rule sets; strict touching is that width at zero)
the five:  [UR] = no contact · [AD] = boundary only · [IN] = partial · [NS] = inside at the edge · [EM] = inside at the core
           (UR, AD, IN are symmetric; NS, EM are directional, subject inside object; identity is excluded because two items are two)

THE RELATION AS A ROW
[relation]      = [activity] whose [object] and [target] are the two [item]s and whose [action] names one of the five, at its [time], under its [rule]
                  (two signed halves, one from each item's pen; a single held half is a finding; never a stored graph)
[frame]         = the [rule] a relation is read under; nothing composes across two frames                                        (no permission bleeds across frames)

COMPOSITION (two relations that share an item)
[composition]   = A-B and B-C [compared] to A-C: the set of states the table allows for A-C, in one frame only
[inference]     = [composition] whose allowed set is one state: the third relation is [derived], never asserted     (inside-core of inside-core is inside-core)
[contradiction] = an asserted A-C [different] from every state the composition allows: a finding, never smoothed
[mediated]      = [derived] relation through a third item: A touches M and B touches M with no A-B row (adjacent through, separated by, connected through)
[role]          = [derived] over an item's relation rows, never a type on the item: boundary object, mediator, corridor, overlap object

WHAT A RELATION MAY BECOME
[zone]          = the edge or core of a host: a qualifier on the relation until a second relation must attach to it, then promoted to an [item]
[overlap item]  = [IN] promoted to an [item] when the shared ground carries its own rules; the rules attach to it, never to A or B   (the owner's ruling: overlap is never a node)
[tree]          = [derived] closure over the directional [NS]/[EM] rows in one frame: an item's parent is what it is inside; many parents lawful; none outside; no loop
[occupancy]     = an [AD] relation instance of an [actor] to a place in a [tree]                                                 (the hat)
[authority]     = a [rule] on roles plus two [occupancy]s, computed; never a relation row

CGL'S THREE RELATION GRAMMARS, each a further split
part            = which part of an [item] a relation or rule scopes: whole | component · surface | inside · wireframe | content
architecture    = how a relation carries influence: hierarchy (an authority line) · network (peers) · system (a flow with feedback)   (gov-os relates to a host as network or system, never hierarchy)
connection      = [relation] of two [activity]s: cause shape (1-1, 1-many, many-1, many-many) x condition (and, or, xor, nand, nor) x timing (finish-start, start-start, finish-finish, start-finish)
```

## Part 3 — every gov-os construct as a combination of the terms above, one line each (no new terms)

```

chain           = each [observation] carries a [comparison] to the previous one; a break is provable at a point
gate            = the one [actor] whose [decision]s enter the [record]; every [draft] crosses it; the only pen
operation       = a [rule] that names a kind of [activity]: its places, its checks, its S/U cell

polarity        = [permission] only: what no rule grants is refused; should/should-not exist only in the human restatement
founding        = the first [rule]s in the [record], before any [event]; the constitution [tier]
power           = the [permission] row; everything outside it is the computed shadow, never a stored list
space           = a [tree] of [NS]/[EM] rows in the organisation frame; every [item] in one; reach includes what is inside
identity        = [derived] over an [actor]'s establishment row, its live sessions and its occupancies (its hats); acts attribute to the establishment through the session
key             = the [row] binding a [secret] to an [actor]; two derived uses: sign and open; revoke is one row
attestation     = the [body] [compares] its own code to the digest in the [record] before its first act

stamp           = a master [derived]'s [comparison] of a [draft] as it climbs: detection, never legitimacy

program         = an [actor] established by a row; its processes are its incarnations; its files its [custody]; its [permission]s derived
glass box       = a [rule]: every program has exactly two openings, to the user and to the system
border          = the system as the one [actor] with no border of its own; an outside request is a [draft]; the reply is signed
handshake       = mutual [comparison] between two [actor]s: who (signature) + what code (attestation) + sane memory (record chain); graded, never all-or-nothing
one pen         = one [record] per observing [actor]; many records per [body] lawful; two actors on one record never
institution     = [rule]s and their stage-role rows under one constitution; a [tree] of institutions; no gate above the boxes
seam            = the closed set of [transformation]s and [move]s the [record] asks a [body] to perform: four object roots x six operations, the twelve kinds of work; empty cells refused by name
witness class   = the [body] that hosts the gate is witnessed by reading its code whole; every other [actor] by its [activity] rows
enclosure       = an [actor] run unmodified inside a [body] with a declared set of asks; every ask a [row]; a refusal in its own native answer
genesis         = the first [observation]s of a [body] at every start: body, chain, constitution read back, derived rebuilt, keys valid, then the first act

brake           = a frozen [derived] holding only should-nots; answers before its row lands; stale fails closed

OMGS stages     = roles [row]s play: environment change (a [different]), monitoring (the [record] + [derived]), analysis (information rows), reporting (a [draft]),
                  decision (the gate), announcement (the record's order), communication (the [unstructured] restatement), enforcement ([compliance]/[breach])
