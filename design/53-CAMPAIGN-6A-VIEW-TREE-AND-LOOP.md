## 1. HEADER — what this is, in one paragraph

Design/25 VERSION 2 states the view tree the owner ruled: four master views, one split per node, level 3 the S/U axis, relationships as venn2 rows. Today only seven masters are seeded rows and every other view is a code function. This campaign makes levels 1 to 3 seeded view rows, gives the relation row its geometry, makes the relation-creating operation live, and publishes the overlap checks the trees feed. Estimate — about five units, two of them founding moves, days not weeks. Confidence Medium: the fence is known, the new folds are counted from the code, the relation row's shape is stated in 25-view-tree-v2.json but not yet built.

## 2. OUTCOME — in the owner's language

Every view the machine keeps permanently is a row in the record that names what it derives from and by which one move, in a tree of four master views whose every node answers a governance question. A relation is one fact with two signed halves that both machines hold, and the same view of it is computed at both ends. Nothing is stored that can be computed, and nothing permanent exists that is only its parent re-indexed.
