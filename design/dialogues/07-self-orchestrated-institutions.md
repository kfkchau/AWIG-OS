## D07.9 — memory constructs identity; genesis is a standing guardian family (owner, 2026-09-05)

**The owner's ruling-class view, his words compressed:** "memory is what
construct identity — the things people have done in the past, what they
have experienced, form their later decisions and choices, which generate
more experience that is unique to them." Identity of entity does NOT
precede memory; memory precedes and constructs identity. His analogy:
the person who wakes feeling like someone else and "regains posture" is
re-deriving self from memory.

**Applied to gov-os — confirmed BY CONSTRUCTION:** the system stores no
self; every view and authority is computed from the record, so identity
re-derives from memory wholly, and a self stored beside the record would
be the wakes-up-confidently-wrong failure.

**The wake checklist (owner's items 2-3; archi's additions 1, 4, 5,
accepted into the frame at the owner's "add if I miss anything"):**
1. verify its own body FIRST (attestation digests — a corrupted
   rememberer verifies memory wrongly; the D07.8 witness-the-code class),
2. verify record integrity (hash chain, end to end),
3. confirm the constitution loaded (founding read back exact),
4. rebuild memory into working shape (views fold FRESH; no cache
   survives sleep on trust),
5. re-derive who it is (own keys valid, account chain intact — C4),
6. only then the first act as an actor. This checklist is the mechanical
   meaning of "it knows it is sane and not crazy."

**The owner's genesis reframe, canon:** on/off and sleep/wake transitions
are genesis functions — genesis is not first-legitimacy only but the
STANDING GUARDIAN of the system's identity-and-memory function at every
gap in consciousness. UNIFIES: boot ceremony (C7, waking intact),
recovery (EP-43, waking damaged — the D06.6 robot), checkpoint views
(EP-44, prepared waking-points), attestation (mover 3, the body-check) —
one family, not four features. ARC R authoring is to proceed under this
frame and cite this record.

**Device layers + the universal actor (the owner's C7 question 3 thread):**
drivers need the common-services toolbox, not Linux; gov-os builds ONE
translator actor impersonating that toolbox — unmodified Linux-compatible
drivers plug in as enclosed workers; every toolbox call crosses the gate
as a row. Scope driven from the narrow device list's real calls, never
built general. Proven pattern elsewhere (trained knowledge, medium
confidence, stated).

## D07.10 — the diving-buddy genesis and the toolbox cut (owner, 2026-09-05)

**The owner's problem statement, canon:** "can an insane person check if
they are sane?" — the self-attestation floor: a broken rememberer can
report itself fine. No single checker escapes this.

**The owner's design, canon — THE DIVING-BUDDY GENESIS:** two processes
with two separate bodies — one from the chip's own memory (BIOS), one
from disk — as part of genesis: (1) each verifies the OTHER's genesis is
clean, (2) each checks the other's genesis code-and-data against its own
copy. "Trust me" becomes "we checked each other, and our copies agree."
Corrupting two checkers identically through two different write-paths is
the harder act — that difficulty is the mechanism.

**What it settles:** the C7 signature-home question (item 1) — the
genesis row records BOTH witnesses' mutual verdicts, a mutual receipt,
not one signer's claim; the chip-side witness is the anchor.

**Unification (third one this dialogue):** EP-45's cross-attestation is
the same mechanism at machine scale. One idea, three sizes: process-pair
at boot, checkpoint cross-attest within a machine (EP-44/45), box-to-box
attestation in C6. ARC R authoring proceeds knowing this and cites here.

**Honest caps, stated:** common-mode failure — one hand writing both
bodies defeats the pair, so the REQUIREMENT is the two bodies never share
a write-path; and beneath both still sits the chip's maker. Smaller
floor, never no floor.

**The driver cut (owner's question, answered his way):** the translator
impersonates ONLY the common driver services — the narrowest waist — and
the bus layer comes in WITH the drivers as enclosed workers (it is
borrowed code of the same kind, written against the same toolbox).
Governance bonus: device discovery becomes a row (device appeared,
matched to driver X) — recorded, where today it is silent.
