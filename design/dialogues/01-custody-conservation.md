## The path, as it actually ran

1. **The owner re-derived item 4's design from the legal side** — AAOT
   shell intact, identifying content removable — before being told it; the
   design and the legal instinct converged independently. (Evidence the
   design sits at the right altitude.)
2. **Architect's humanity position:** forgetting is one of humanity's
   oldest mercy technologies (amnestia, jubilees, spent convictions); the
   two dishonest forms are soft-delete (surveillance in a courtesy veil)
   and total erasure (the memory-hole); the honest form is forget the
   content, remember the forgetting — mercy with a receipt. Cap stated: no
   system makes the world forget; the honest maximum is "we no longer hold
   it, and here is the proof we let it go."
3. **The owner rejected the settled frame entirely and designed past it**
   (verbatim, the design): "can we have a function duplicate the record
   chain of that person … verify byte to byte full record transfer before
   deleted aaot content … row item shell has to remain … the system delete
   line by line each line verification as a second pass … the duplicated
   personal related data chain is on a different ownership hand … the
   system will never bear responsibility of removing history, it can split
   out, but cannot delete."
4. **Architect's assessment:** better than the built design — deletion
   becomes CUSTODY TRANSFER; the invariant "we never remove information
   from the world, we only change whose hands hold it" is stable across
   every legal regime; the machinery is EP-43's recover ceremony run in
   reverse; item 1's keys make the receipt provable. One position held
   back: a narrow DESTROY ceremony retained for content no one may hold.
5. **The owner escalated to law** (verbatim): "handover is mix, there is
   no such function as delete. Must be law for this dev, for humanity
   (humanity beyond human alone … longevity of co-intelligence
   civilisation with ai x ai, ai x human, human x human, being x being
   …), we are bearing a much much much much greater responsibility here."
6. **The architect FOLDED the narrow-destroy position, reasons on the
   record:** any delete function, however narrow, is the door a future
   authority widens (the exception becomes the road — the K2 shape at
   civilizational scale); at AI×AI speed a memory-hole operates at machine
   speed; and by the estate's own enforcement hierarchy,
   capability-absence beats every promise — a system whose vocabulary has
   no delete cannot be compelled to delete.

## The law it produced (board :2856, operative form)

Information custody is conserved: it moves between hands, it is never
annihilated by this system. There is no delete function and the vocabulary
will never contain one. The one function answering every removal demand is
verified custody transfer: duplicate the chain, verify byte-to-byte,
receive the signed receipt (keys as prerequisite), remove the local copy
line-by-line verified, the row shells and a DEPARTURE record standing
forever. Whoever receives acts thereafter at their own hand, under their
own law, outside this system. Constitutional-class: carried into every
future world's founding. Deployment boundary stated honestly: a
jurisdiction refusing we-transfer-cannot-delete is a place this system
does not deploy — a boundary, never a function.

## Why this is civilizational, in the owner's frame

Every dark age is a memory-destruction event. Human civilization survived
its book-burnings because memory was distributed and slow to burn; a
co-intelligence civilization on shared record substrates has a single
throat unless the substrate class has none. This law removes the throat —
and because this architecture is a template, refusing the delete function
here refuses it in every system that copies the template. The design
outlives the legal regimes it will meet, which was the owner's stated
requirement: societal-structure-evolution-proof.
