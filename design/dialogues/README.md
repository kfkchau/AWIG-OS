# design/dialogues — the humanity-engineering record

**What this folder is.** The owner's meta-architecture contribution arrives
as DIALOGUE — compressed questions, derivations pushed against the
architect, corrections in both directions — and the filed laws are only its
residue. This folder keeps the GENERATOR: each record preserves a dialogue's
derivation path, corrections unsmoothed, the owner's verbatim key wordings
(they are canon per OWNER-COMMS-STANDARD), and what law or design it
produced. By the owner's own criterion (a frame that can explain other
frames is the more correct one), this is the estate's most valuable layer:
it shows the frame PRODUCING answers, not just the answers.

**Why a folder and not the board:** the board records acts one line at a
time; a dialogue is an ARC — question to law — and its value is the path.
Board lines cite these records; these records cite the board.

**The record format:** date · participants · the opening question verbatim ·
the derivation path as it actually ran (including positions later folded,
per preserve-don't-smooth) · the owner's gold wordings verbatim · what it
produced (laws by board coordinate, designs by number) · open threads.

**Atomic addressing (owner-ruled 2026-09-04):** each dialogue is D<nn>
(D01, D02 …); each numbered path step is citable as D<nn>.<step> (D01.5 is
the step where custody-conservation was spoken into law). **Citation is
LAW, not habit: any design doc, law, or EP whose WHAT originated in a
dialogue cites its D-address in the provenance line** — an uncited law is
a retrieved claim at corpus scale. No tooling until the count justifies it;
the docmap inventory may grow a dangling-D-ref check later.

**Standing rule:** the architect writes the record within a day of the
dialogue, while the path is exact; the owner corrects; his corrections
control. A dialogue that produced law gets its record BEFORE the law is
built on.
